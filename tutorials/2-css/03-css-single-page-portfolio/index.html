My Portfolio

Hi! I'm an artist currently pursuing my Bachelor's degree in Digital Media Art at San José State University. My artistic journey began with a love for drawing and painting, but over time, my curiosity has inspired me to explore different mediums and creative techniques. I enjoy creating artwork that tells a story, expresses emotions, and allows people to connect with my ideas. I find inspiration in everyday life, the people around me, and the little details that often go unnoticed. As an artist, I believe creativity is about experimenting, making mistakes, and continuously growing. My goal is to keep developing my artistic skills while exploring my passion for animation and storytelling.
